Seadrill Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

November 3, 2026 

HAMILTON, Bermuda--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- 
Seadrill Limited (“Seadrill” or the “Company”) (NYSE: SDRL) today announced its third quarter 2026 results.
Highlights
Secured contract awards and extensions in Indonesia, Malaysia, Angola, and the U.S. Gulf, adding approximately $800 million of Total Contract Value (1) subsequent to the August fleet status report.
Generated $195 million of Net cash provided by operating activities, $179 million of Free Cash Flow (2), and repurchased approximately $22 million of shares in the third quarter.
Reported Net loss of $30 million, which includes the impact of a legal provision booked in the third quarter, and Adjusted EBITDA (2) of $121 million.
Increased and narrowed full year 2026 Total operating revenues and Adjusted EBITDA (3) guidance ranges as follows:Total operating revenues range increased to $1.56 - $1.58 billion, excluding $90 million of Reimbursable revenues (previously $1.50 - $1.55 billion, excluding $50 million of Reimbursable revenues);
Adjusted EBITDA (3) range increased to $440 - $460 million (previously $420 - $450 million);
Capital Expenditure and Long-Term Maintenance range narrowed to $210 - $230 million (previously $200 - $240 million).

“Seadrill generated $179 million of Free Cash Flow in the third quarter, delivering on our commitment to convert backlog into cash. Over 85% of available drillship days are now contracted in 2027, further enhancing visibility into next year’s earnings and cash flow,” said Samir Ali, President and Chief Executive Officer. “The West Carina award secures the third and final repricing of three legacy contracts, with the improved dayrate effective in 2027. A healthy tender pipeline and growing deepwater demand reinforce our confidence in the market outlook, positioning Seadrill to build on these commercial achievements and capture further upside.”
Financial and Operational Results
Third quarter 2026 Total operating revenues decreased to $428 million, compared to $449 million in the prior quarter, primarily driven by fewer operating days for the West Carina, which completed its contract in the second quarter, and time between contracts for the West Vela, West Elara, and Sevan Louisiana. This decrease was partially offset by an increase in operating days for the West Tellus, which commenced its long-term contract in June. Total operating expenses increased by $7 million to $384 million, compared to $377 million in the prior quarter. Adjusted EBITDA decreased to $121 million, compared to $144 million in the prior quarter.
Balance Sheet and Cash Flow
At quarter-end, Seadrill had gross principal debt of $750 million and $506 million in cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash, resulting in a net debt position of $244 million. Third quarter 2026 cash benefited from the receipt of lump-sum mobilization revenue relating to the West Tellus and a working capital release. Capital additions and long-term maintenance totaled $56 million.
Commercial Activity
Subsequent to the August fleet status report, Seadrill has secured contract awards and extensions with a Total Contract Value of approximately $800 million.
In Indonesia, the West Carina was awarded a contract with an undisclosed operator, with a Total Contract Value of approximately $420 million for the firm term. The contract has an estimated firm duration of 880 days, with priced options for up to an additional 880 days. The program is expected to commence in the first half of 2027.
In Malaysia, all remaining priced options have been exercised for the West Capella, extending current operations by 120 days and adding approximately $41 million in Total Contract Value, excluding additional services. Unpriced options have also been exercised for a further 120 days, adding approximately $53 million in Total Contract Value. The West Capella is now expected to work in direct continuation through February 2028.
In Angola, Sonadrill Holding Ltd (“Sonadrill”), Seadrill’s 50:50 joint venture with an affiliate of Sonangol E.P. (“Sonangol”), has been awarded a contract extension. All remaining priced options have been exercised for the Sonangol Libongos, extending current operations by approximately three years and committing the rig into 2030.
In the U.S. Gulf, the Sevan Louisiana was awarded a 25-day contract with Kosmos, commencing in November 2026, followed by an approximate 60-day contract with LLOG, securing the rig into 2027.
As of November 3, 2026, Seadrill’s Contract Backlog(4) was approximately $3.3 billion. The Company has provided an updated fleet status report on the Investor Relations section of its website, www.seadrill.com.

Appendix I - Reconciliation of Net (loss)/income to Adjusted EBITDA (Unaudited)
Adjusted EBITDA represents Net (loss)/income before depreciation and amortization, income tax expense, total financial and non-operating items, and similar non-cash charges. Additionally, in any given period, the Company may have significant, unusual or non-recurring items which may be excluded from Adjusted EBITDA for that period. When applicable, these items are fully disclosed and incorporated into the reconciliation provided below. Adjusted EBITDA Margin represents Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of Total operating revenues. Adjusted EBITDA excluding Reimbursables, represents Adjusted EBITDA, excluding Reimbursable revenues and Reimbursable expenses. Adjusted EBITDA Margin excluding Reimbursables represents Adjusted EBITDA excluding Reimbursables as a percentage of Total operating revenues excluding Reimbursable revenues.
Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted EBITDA excluding Reimbursables and Adjusted EBITDA Margin excluding Reimbursables are non-GAAP financial measures. The Company believes that the aforementioned non-GAAP financial measures assist investors by excluding the potentially disparate effects between periods of depreciation and amortization, income tax expense and total financial items and non-operating items, which are affected by various and possibly changing financing methods, capital structure and historical cost basis and which may significantly affect Net (loss)/income between periods.
Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted EBITDA excluding Reimbursables and Adjusted EBITDA Margin excluding Reimbursables should not be considered as alternatives to Net (loss)/income or any other indicator of Seadrill Limited’s performance calculated in accordance with GAAP. Because the definitions of Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted EBITDA excluding Reimbursables and Adjusted EBITDA Margin excluding Reimbursables (or similar measures) may vary among companies and industries, they may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures used by other companies.

(4) Economic utilization is defined as dayrate revenue earned during the period, excluding bonuses, divided by the contractual operating dayrate, multiplied by the number of days on contract in the period. If a drilling unit earns its full operating dayrate throughout a reporting period, its economic utilization would be 100%. However, there are many situations that give rise to a dayrate being earned that is less than the contractual operating rate, such as planned downtime for maintenance. In such situations, economic utilization reduces below 100%.
